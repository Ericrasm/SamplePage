Who We Are

A Security Partner You Can Trust

BES Security has been delivering exceptional security solutions since 2001. We work closely with businesses of all sizes — from small retail outlets to large commercial complexes — crafting tailored security programmes that are both effective and cost-efficient.

Our team of fully vetted, SIA-licensed officers is supported by a state-of-the-art control room and a dedicated management team available around the clock.

Fully Licensed & Insured — all officers hold valid SIA licences and our company carries comprehensive public liability insurance.
Bespoke Security Plans — we never apply a one-size-fits-all approach; every client receives a customised security strategy.
ISO 9001 Quality Management — our processes are independently audited to ensure consistently high standards of service delivery.
24/7 Management Support — a senior manager is always on call to handle any escalation swiftly and professionally.
